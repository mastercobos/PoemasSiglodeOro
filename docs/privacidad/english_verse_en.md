# English Verse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

English Verse is an app developed by Manuel Cobos Robles. This privacy policy describes how the app handles your data.

## Data we collect

English Verse does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no user registration, no activity tracking and no connection to external servers.

## Data stored locally

The app stores only the following on your device:

- The poems you mark as favourites
- The authors you select in the settings
- The app's theme
- Whether the daily reminder is on, and at what time
- Recently shown authors, to vary the poems of the day

This data is never sent to us or to third parties, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. If your device's backups are turned on (for example, Google or iCloud), they may include this data and restore it later. Those backups belong to your account and we have no access to them.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications where your device requires it. Reminders are scheduled on your own device, using its clock and time zone, and do not pass through any server. You can turn them off at any time.

## Android permissions

On Android, English Verse requests only these permissions:

- **Show notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): to show the daily reminder. Android 13 and later ask you first, and you can read the poems without granting it.
- **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): to schedule reminders again after a restart, because Android clears them.
- **Control vibration** (`VIBRATE`): so a reminder can vibrate, according to your device's notification settings.

Android's libraries also add an internal permission (`com.manucobos.englishverse.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`), which protects communication within the app and gives access to nothing else.

None of these permissions gives access to your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone.

## Sharing poems

When you share a poem, the app creates an image or text and hands it to the app you choose. From then on, that app's privacy policy applies. We receive no information about what you share.

## Internet connection

English Verse does not need an internet connection and does not communicate with any external server. On Android, it does not even request the internet permission. The link to this policy in the settings opens in your browser, outside the app.
